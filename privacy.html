Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Clipper King Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Clipper King channels to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and Facebook accounts. It is not available to the public.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the Clipper King channel and to read that channel's basic details and video list.
  • Meta (Instagram/Facebook): permission to publish videos to the Clipper King Page and Instagram account.

How data is used and stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to publish and check the owner's own videos. No personal data of any other person is collected. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at Google Account permissions or in Meta's Business Integrations settings.
